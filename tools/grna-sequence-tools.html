gRNA Sequence Tools

Analyze guide sequence properties and identify candidate spacer sites using protein-specific PAM/TAM motifs or custom IUPAC patterns.

Mode 1 · Guide sequence checker

Default: 45–65%. You can change either limit to suit your system.

Mode 2 · PAM / TAM site scanner

Select a nuclease system or define a custom PAM/TAM. The scanner searches both DNA strands and reports candidate spacer sequences in 5′ → 3′ orientation.

Default: 20 nt; adjustable from 1–100 nt.
Use A/C/G/T and IUPAC ambiguity codes, e.g. N, R, W, B, D, H, K, M, S, V, Y.
OgeuIscB
5′-NNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNNN-NWRRNA-3′
TAM is located at the 3′ side of the spacer.
IUPAC / degenerate base codes: B = C/G/T; D = A/G/T; H = A/C/T; K = G/T; M = A/C; N = A/C/G/T; R = A/G; S = C/G; V = A/C/G; W = A/T; Y = C/T. These codes are supported in PAM/TAM motifs and guide-sequence checking.
Interpretation: GC range and homopolymer flags are simple sequence heuristics only. They do not establish guide efficacy, specificity, editing outcome, or safety. Confirm candidates with appropriate genome-wide off-target analysis and experimental validation.
