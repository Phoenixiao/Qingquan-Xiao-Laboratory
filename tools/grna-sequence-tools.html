gRNA Sequence Tools

Check guide RNA sequence properties and scan target DNA for selected CRISPR nuclease PAM motifs.

Mode 1 · Guide sequence checker

Mode 2 · PAM site scanner

Scan an input DNA target for candidate protospacers adjacent to a selected PAM on both strands. This is a sequence-pattern scan, not an off-target or activity prediction.

Interpretation: GC range and homopolymer flags are simple sequence heuristics only. They do not establish guide efficacy, specificity, editing outcome, or safety. Confirm candidates with appropriate genome-wide off-target analysis and experimental validation.
