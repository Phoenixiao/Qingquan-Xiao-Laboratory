Primer Design Tool

Standalone browser-based PCR primer design. Enter a single DNA sequence or multiple FASTA records, adjust primer and product parameters, and evaluate candidate primer pairs locally in your browser.

1. Input sequence

Single sequence accepts either a plain DNA sequence or one FASTA record. Batch FASTA requires FASTA headers (>) and supports up to 20 records. Maximum sequence length: 20,000 nt per record. Whitespace and digits are ignored.

2. Primer parameters

Allowed range: 15–35 nt
For example, AAAAA has length 5
Default values are typical starting points, not universal optima. Minimum and maximum values are hard filters; optimal values contribute to the ranking penalty.

3. PCR product parameters

Products within the optimal range receive no product-size penalty. Products outside that range may still be returned if they satisfy the minimum and maximum product-size limits.

4. Reaction and structure parameters

Maximum output: 15 pairs per sequence
Important model limitation: Self-any, Self-3′, Pair-any and Pair-3′ are longest contiguous complementary-match indicators. They are not Primer3 thermodynamic alignment scores, ΔG values, SELF_ANY_TH or SELF_END_TH. Tm uses a nearest-neighbor calculation with a simplified monovalent salt correction. No genome-wide off-target specificity search is performed.
