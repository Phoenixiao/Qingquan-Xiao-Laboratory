Sequence Utilities

DNA, RNA and protein sequence analysis, six-frame translation, ORF identification, codon usage analysis, and host-specific codon optimization.

Input sequence

Reading frame is 1-based. +1, +2 and +3 begin at nucleotide 1, 2 and 3, respectively.
A codon is flagged when its frequency is below this fraction of the most frequent synonymous codon.
Weighted selection distributes synonymous codons according to their relative frequencies.

Result

Genetic code: Translation uses NCBI Standard Genetic Code (translation table 1). For bacterial ORF discovery, an optional set of common bacterial initiation codons is available. Alternative initiation codons are translated as methionine only at the beginning of a predicted ORF.
CAI interpretation: CAI is calculated as the geometric mean of relative synonymous codon adaptiveness. Methionine and tryptophan are excluded because each has only one codon. Stop codons are evaluated separately using their relative frequencies. This is a reference-set-dependent score, not a direct measurement of expression.
Experimental design: Codon optimization does not guarantee increased expression. This tool does not model mRNA secondary structure, translation initiation context, tRNA abundance, codon-pair effects, cryptic splice sites, restriction sites, repeats, toxicity, or protein folding. Always verify the final sequence and experimental design.
